= PPLNS Share Accounting
:page-parent: Sharechain
:page-nav_order: 30

Each share has enough data to reconstruct the bitcoin block's coinbase
transaction. Given the payout distribution in the bitcoin coinbase
p2poolv2 nodes compare it to the payout distribution derived from the
local view of the sharechain. If these match, the share is valid, else
it is rejected with a PPLNS mismatch error.

== Coinbase Maturity, PPLNS Window and Trading Window

The N in the pay-per-last-N-shares in P2Poolv2 is 120,960 which maps
to the number of target block interval periods of 10s in a two week
window.

A coinbase can only be spent once it is 6048 deep from the
chain tip. This is chosen to be the same time period as 100 bitcoin
blocks. So the PPLNS window starts after the first 6048 blocks on the
chain, that is the maturity window shares are excluded from the PPLNS
accounting.

The window shares between coinbase maturity and the PPLNS expiry
height is called the trading window. The diagram below shows these
concepts.

== Uncle Blocks and Rewards

Each shareblock header can refer to maximum of three uncle blocks, and
each uncle referenced from a shareblock should not be more than three
blocks deep from itself. A shareblock referenced as an uncle by a
shareblock cannot be referenced as an uncle shareblock from another
shareblock.


[graphviz, uncle-blocks, svg]
.Shareblock 4 references two uncles

  s4 -> u2 [style=dashed, color="#FFFFFFC7", constraint=false,

  s4 -> u3 [style=dashed, color="#FFFFFFC7", constraint=false,
            label="uncle"]

....

In the diagram, Uncle A and Uncle B are valid shares that lost the
race to join the main chain. Uncle A and uncle B are both uncles of
Shareblock 4.

PPLNS accounting counts an uncle's work in full but splits it between
two miners:

* The uncle's miner earns 90% of the uncle's difficulty.
* The nephew's miner earns the nephew's own difficulty plus 10% of each
  uncle's difficulty.

Suppose every share in the diagram has difficulty _d_. The miners of
Uncle A and Uncle B each earn 0.9__d__, and the miner of Shareblock 4
earns 1.2__d__: its own _d_ plus 0.1__d__ for each uncle. Together they
earn 3__d__, the same as three shares on the main chain, so no work goes
uncounted. The bonus gives miners a reason to include uncles, and the
discount keeps a share on the main chain worth more than an uncle.
