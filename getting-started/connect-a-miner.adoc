= Connect a miner
:page-parent: Getting started
:page-nav_order: 2

Point any Stratum v1 miner at your node's stratum port.

[source,text]
----
stratum+tcp://<your-node>:3333
----

. Open your miner's pool settings.
. Set the pool URL to the address above.
. Set the worker name to your bitcoin address.
. Save, and watch the node log for accepted shares.
