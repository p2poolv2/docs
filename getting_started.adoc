= Getting Started
:page-nav_order: 2
:page-has_children: true

A P2Poolv2 node runs a Stratum v1 server for your miners and a p2p
network node that builds a share chain with in collaboration with
other p2poolv2 nodes. The share chain is a blockchain of weak bitcoin
blocks that supports blocks. See
xref:architecture/sharechain.adoc[Sharechain Design] for details on how the
share chain works.

The best place to see how to run a node is the
https://github.com/p2poolv2/p2poolv2/blob/main/README.adoc[README on
our repository].

== Testnet4 is Live

You can see what the p2poolv2's testnet4 pool is doing by look at our
https://grafana.p2poolv2.org[Grafana dashboard] and our node's
https://testnet4.p2poolv2.org[view of the share chain].

If you want to mine to our public node, you can connect to
`stratum://testnet4.p2poolv2.org:3333`. Even though we recommend running
a node. P2Poolv2 is only as strong as there are nodes mining on it.
