P2Poolv2 is a permissionless mining pool where miners build block templates,
continuously audit accounting, and are paid directly to their wallets.

P2Poolv2 runs a sharechain of weak bitcoin blocks, where the
sharechain runs the Nakamoto consensus with a weaker target than
bitcoin. The is able to run share accounting in a decentralized
manner - all nodes have reach eventual consistency on the sharechain
accounting and can validate all shares and payouts.

The following pages describe the requirements and design decisions of
P2Poolv2.
